About ALLURED VIRTUE

The words
have a home.

AV

ALLURED VIRTUE is the home of works by Donesha Park. It exists so each manuscript can remain accessible in its digital form, without requiring a reader to purchase entry into the words.

The work moves through remembrance, identity, love, perception, pain, creation, motherhood, dreams, boundaries, and the truth beneath the names placed upon us.

“All digital formats remain free.”

Why free?

A price can support a physical object—the paper, printing, binding, and labor of a hard copy. It does not have to stand between a reader and a digital manuscript. The library is built around that distinction.

A living library

This website will grow as the work grows. Finished PDFs will be added when they are ready. Future pages, audio readings, and physical editions can be added later without changing the purpose of this home.

The author

Donesha Park is a writer and singer whose work follows the process of remembering while it is still unfolding. The manuscripts preserve the living movement of becoming.
