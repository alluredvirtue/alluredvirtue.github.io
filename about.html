From the Founder

Everything begins with consciousness.

Consciousness is not something to attain, awaken, purify, or earn. It is.

Before language, identity, religion, spirituality, science, philosophy, and thought, there is awareness. That awareness is untouched by every definition created after it.

It is not imposed, inherited, or named. There are no labels, systems, words, scripts, or man-made facts. The void is the space where everything accepted without consciousness is released; where impossible is available because nothing remains to declare it impossible.

There is no search because nothing is missing. There is no dependence because nothing outside of consciousness completes it.

Thought is a tool, it has no authority unless it is accepted, repeated, fed, and sustained.

The mind is strong, but consciousness is sovereign.

To abandon everything and remain void is to remain in nothingness. Life is meant to be lived. Once everything false has been removed, reality can be molded into what brings joy.

Every mind is different. Grace is vital.

Some recognize what they are immediately. Others must question, research, experience, dismantle, and understand before they can release what they inherited. The journey does not create consciousness, but it can reveal what obscured it.

I study because I yearn to understand humans, the mind, myself, reality, language, religion, spirituality, symbols, dreams, nature, creation, darkness, light, pain, energy, origin, essence and the unknown.

Nothing must be rejected before it is understood.

My life is the expression of what I remember.

To know is to know SELF.
